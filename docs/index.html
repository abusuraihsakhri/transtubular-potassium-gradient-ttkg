Equations and evidence

TTKG = (urine K × plasma osmolality) / (urine osmolality × plasma K). The historical prerequisites include urine osmolality ≥ plasma osmolality and urine sodium ≥ 25 mEq/L, but meeting them does not resolve the limitations from medullary urea recycling.

Spot urine K/Cr (mEq/g creatinine) = urine K (mEq/L) ÷ urine creatinine (mg/dL) × 100. In hypokalemia, a ratio above 13 mEq/g suggests renal potassium wasting; it is not definitive.

References: Kamel & Halperin, Kidney International Reports (2021) · Palmer & Clegg, CJASN (2019) · Kardalas et al., Endocrine Connections (2018).
